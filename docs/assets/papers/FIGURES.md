# Figure credits

The research project figures retain their original dimensions. The selected publication thumbnails are 720 pixels wide. All figures preserve their proportions, with transparent areas rendered on white.

## Game-enhanced pretraining

Lianlei Shan, Han Jin, Yaunchao Ma, Xinpeng Chen, Linhao Zhou, and Xin Bin, *Game-Enhanced Embodied Learning with Event and Foresight Pretraining* (arXiv 2611.13819v1). Figure 1 illustrates how MPCoT combines several latent reasoning paths before decoding an action. The homepage uses a WebP conversion of the authors’ figure. ## AVA-VLA

Dianqiao Lei and Lianlei Shan, *Think Less, Act Early: Reinforced Latent Reasoning with Early Exit in Vision-Language-Action Models* (ICML 2026). ## LTPO

[ltpo.webp](./ltpo.webp) · 720 × 577 px

Wengao Ye, Yan Liang, and Lianlei Shan, *Thinking on the Fly: Test-Time Reasoning Enhancement via Latent Thought Policy Optimization* (ICLR 2026). Figure 1 shows how a reward derived from the frozen model’s output updates the latent thought embeddings before answer generation. The thumbnail was rendered from the authors’ SVG. ## F2Net

[f2net.webp](./f2net.webp) · 720 × 343 px

Hengzhi Chen, Liqian Feng, Wenhua Wu, Xiaogang Zhu, Qiuxia Wu, Lianlei Shan, and Kun Hu, *F2Net: A Frequency-Fused Network for Ultra-High Resolution Remote Sensing Segmentation* (CVPR 2026). An aerial image is split into high- and low-frequency branches, then their features are fused to produce a segmentation map. © 2026 The F2Net Authors. ## DeepLatent Reasoning (DLR)

[dlr.webp](./dlr.webp) · 720 × 480 px

Lianlei Shan, Han Chen, Yixuan Wang, Zhenjie Liu, and Wei Li, *Latent-Space Contrastive Reinforcement Learning for Stable and Efficient LLM Reasoning* (arXiv 2601.17275v1, 2026). Figure 1 shows how an assistant samples latent reasoning trajectories, how rewards filter them for decoding by a frozen model, and how contrastive training updates the assistant.
